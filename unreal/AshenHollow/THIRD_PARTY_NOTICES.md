# Rules attribution

## Epic Games template assets

`Content/Characters/Mannequins` contains 128 unmodified files from the locally installed Unreal Engine 5.8.2 `Templates/TemplateResources/High/Characters/Content/Mannequins` resource pack (Epic Games). These character meshes, materials, textures, rigs and animations are Epic template content, not original Ashen Hollow artwork and not covered by the SRD Creative Commons license. They are used for the Unreal movement prototype.

This work includes material taken from the System Reference Document 5.1 (“SRD 5.1”) by Wizards of the Coast LLC and available at https://dnd.wizards.com/resources/systems-reference-document. The SRD 5.1 is licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License available at https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode.

Rules have been adapted into executable real-time mechanics and concise original descriptions. The supported subset and changes are documented in docs/FIFTH_EDITION.md. The game world, interface, procedural geometry and synthesized sounds are original project content.
