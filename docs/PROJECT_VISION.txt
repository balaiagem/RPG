* Dungeons & Dragons 5e / SRD-style tabletop rules

# CORE GAMEPLAY

The game is an isometric action RPG.

The player sees the world from an elevated three-quarter perspective.

Movement works like a modern MOBA:

RIGHT CLICK:
Move the character toward the clicked location.

RIGHT CLICK ON ENEMY:
Move into appropriate range and perform the currently equipped basic attack.

Q, W, E, R:
Primary combat abilities/spells.

1, 2, 3, 4:
Consumables or secondary abilities.

LEFT CLICK:
Interaction, UI selection, inventory management, dialogue choices and target selection when appropriate.

SPACE:
Optional dodge / contextual action depending on character abilities.

TAB:
Highlight interactable objects and enemies.

SHIFT:
Hold position while attacking/casting when useful.

ESC:
Pause/settings menu.

The movement needs to feel extremely responsive.

Implement:

* navigation/pathfinding
* click destination indicator
* character acceleration/deceleration
* animation blending
* enemy targeting
* attack range detection
* ability range indicators
* ground-targeted abilities
* projectile abilities
* skillshots
* area-of-effect abilities
* melee attacks
* casting animations
* cooldown indicators
* health bars
* mana/resource bars
* status effects
* damage numbers
* critical hit feedback

Combat should have the readability and responsiveness of a competitive isometric game while retaining RPG depth.

# REAL-TIME ADAPTATION OF 5E

Do NOT turn combat into traditional turn-based combat.

Instead adapt tabletop mechanics intelligently to real-time combat.

For example:

One tabletop round = approximately 6 seconds.

Actions, bonus actions and reactions should be represented by cooldowns, resource restrictions or contextual opportunities.

Example:

An ability that would normally require an Action can use the global combat cadence.

Bonus-action-type abilities should generally have shorter or independent usage restrictions.

Reactions should become contextual triggers or reactive abilities.

Preserve the strategic identity of the tabletop rules while making them enjoyable in real-time.
